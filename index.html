What it does

  • Answers customers instantly, on Messenger or SMS now, WhatsApp as it comes online — wherever they already text you.
  • Reads a photo of the job and responds naturally, not a generic "someone will get back to you."
  • Books a real slot on your real calendar, no back-and-forth.
  • Checks whether the job's even in your service area before you waste time on it.
  • Everything reviewable before it sends, until you trust it enough to let it run itself.

Why this exists

Hey — if you run a home-service business, you already know this problem better than I could ever explain it to you. A customer texts a photo of their garage at 9pm. Someone asks if you service their zip code. Someone wants to know if Tuesday works. And you're the one answering all of it, every time, on top of actually doing the work.

This started because a buddy of mine who runs a home-service business was missing time with his family because he couldn't put his phone down. I built TowerThreadAI to fix that for him — the first response, reading the photo to keep things moving, the real booking against his real calendar — so the only messages that actually need him are the ones that really do.

When I told him the whole vision — start to finish, the first response all the way to booking, eventually invoicing — his reaction stuck with me: this is going to replace me. He meant it as the highest compliment a guy who works with his hands can give. It replaced the version of him that couldn't get away from his phone, not the version that shows up for his family.
